Since 1986

Pretend it’s 1986.

That’s what the chalkboard says, and we mean it. Front Porch has been family owned and operated in Dunnellon since 1986: real plates, real gravy, and pie that comes out of the oven every morning.

Pull up a chair inside or out on the porch, catch some live music, and stay a while. Groups and families are always welcome. Just call ahead for big parties.
