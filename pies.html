Pie questions

How much notice do you need for a whole pie?

Please give us a day’s notice. We bake every morning and pies are out of the oven by 11:00 AM, so you can pick up any day we’re open from 11 AM on.

Do you ship or deliver pies?

Not right now. Pies are for local pickup only at 12039 N Florida Ave in Dunnellon.

How much is a whole pie?

$24 for any of our 14 regular flavors. Carrot cake, chocolate cake and Cherry Bo pie are $45 whole when available. Mini pies are $8 ($10 for pecan and Cherry Bo). Menu prices are cash prices and may change.

Can I pick up on a Monday?

We’re closed Mondays. Pickups are Tuesday through Sunday, after 11 AM.

Can I just stop in for a slice?

Absolutely. Slices are $6.49, add a scoop of ice cream for $2. Whatever’s in the case that day is yours, dine-in or to go.
